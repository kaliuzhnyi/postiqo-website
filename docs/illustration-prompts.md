## Hero generation prompt

Create one premium website hero illustration for Postiqo Publisher, a service for automotive dealerships that automatically publishes vehicle inventory to four marketplaces. This is a finished production image asset for a light blue and white landing page.

ART DIRECTION: Sophisticated flat 2D editorial vector-like illustration, with crisp clean geometric silhouettes, carefully balanced negative space, subtle thin navy linework and restrained light blue background shapes. The visual language is a polished modern evolution of friendly unDraw-style SaaS illustrations. Absolutely NOT a 3D render, not glossy, not photorealistic, not a screenshot, not a generic office scene. Use a strictly cohesive palette of vivid blue #0d83fd, deep slate navy #2d465e, pale blue #e8f3ff, cool light gray and white, with tiny mint green success accents. Transparent background. No large white enclosing rectangle.

SUBJECT AND STORY: One dealership inventory becomes four active sales channels, without manual work. Make a beautiful contemporary blue SUV the main foreground focal point, with recognizable elegant automotive proportions, tasteful dark windows and wheels, and a smaller white sedan behind it. Place them in front of one simplified inventory source panel on the left, containing three clean vehicle thumbnail rows. To the right, exactly four independent compact vehicle listing cards are arranged as an airy 2 by 2 cluster. Each card contains an attractive small stylized vehicle image, a blue information bar, two small gray metadata bars, and a tiny green published check. An elegant thin blue connecting route branches from the inventory source to the four cards. A small blue automation hub with a circular refresh icon sits at the branch, communicating that the inventory is kept synchronized. Optional one tasteful minimalist standing dealership manager at the far left, no laptop and no desk. The cars and connected listing cards are the heroes, people are secondary.

COMPOSITION: Wide landscape 3:2 canvas, transparent background, illustration fills about 88 percent of the canvas with safe breathing room. A clear single coherent scene, not an infographic full of symbols, and not a collage. Foreground vehicle occupies the lower-left and center, marketplace cards occupy the upper-right. Keep the overall silhouette compact and visually balanced so it reads beautifully at 600 pixels wide on a website. Ground everything with a few pale blue flat oval shapes and subtle thin lines only, no realistic shadows or gradients.

CONSTRAINTS: No text, no letters, no numbers, no platform logos, no watermark, no headline. No robots, rockets, megaphones, coins, charts, giant gears or generic tech clip art. No excessive tiny interface details. No glossy reflections. The result must feel purpose-designed for a trustworthy automotive inventory automation product and appeal to a professional car dealership owner.

CONSTRAINTS: No checkerboard pattern and no simulated transparency. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. No laptop, no desk, no robot, no giant gear, no generic corporate meeting.
