Before we connect

Your website questions, answered.

Do I need to change my DMS?

No. Postiqo Website is a customer-facing dealership website, not a replacement DMS. Your existing DMS or inventory source remains the source of truth.

Will my dealership need to enter inventory twice?

No. We connect the website to a supported inventory source so your team can keep managing vehicles in its existing system. Pricing, mileage, availability and vehicle information sync according to the data that source provides.

Can you connect to my DMS?

Tell us which DMS, API, inventory feed or other inventory source you use. We review the available access and data before confirming a DMS integrated dealership website. Available fields, update frequency and sold vehicle removal depend on the connected source.

Do I need Postiqo Publisher to use Website?

No. Postiqo Website is a standalone product. You can keep your existing inventory system and request a website quote without a Publisher subscription.

What does a dealership inventory website cost?

Starting at C$3,500 for one-time setup, plus C$299/month for hosting, SSL, inventory synchronization, maintenance, backups, platform updates and standard support. Complex or custom DMS integrations may require additional setup work and will be quoted separately. All prices are in Canadian dollars.
