Before we get started

A few useful answers.

Who is Postiqo Pages for?

Pages is for local businesses that need a simple website to introduce their services and receive enquiries. It suits trades, salons, studios, repair shops, local professionals and other service businesses.

Can I have more than a landing page?

Yes. We can build a single landing page or a simple website with separate pages, such as About us, Services, How we work, Gallery, Location and Contact. We agree on the structure and price before the build.

What does it cost?

The website build starts at C$1,000 as a one-time payment. Hosting is free. Your domain is paid separately at its registration and renewal cost, with no Postiqo markup or commission. The final quote depends on the pages and content you need. All prices are in Canadian dollars.

Is hosting really free?

Yes. Hosting is included at no charge with Postiqo Pages. Your domain registration and renewal are paid separately, and the cost depends on the domain you choose.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. We can connect your existing domain to the new website. You continue paying its renewal cost. If you need a new domain, we help you choose one and pass on its cost without markup or commission.

How is Pages different from Postiqo Website?

Pages presents a local business and its services through simple pages. Postiqo Website is for car dealerships that need vehicle inventory connected to an existing DMS or supported inventory source. Pages is a standalone product and does not require a Publisher subscription.
