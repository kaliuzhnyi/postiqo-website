Postiqo Cards FAQ

Before you print your first QR.

What is a digital vehicle window sticker?

It is a printed QR code that opens a vehicle page on a buyer's phone. Postiqo Cards shows the current inventory price, photos, specifications, full description and your dealership's contact details.

Do I need to print a new QR code when the price changes?

No. The vehicle link stays the same when the online price changes. Use a QR sign without a printed price to avoid reprinting it for price updates. Any fixed price or other information printed on paper still needs to be kept accurate.

How does the price stay current?

Cards reads the latest inventory information synchronized to Postiqo. A price change in your source inventory must sync first. New visits then show the updated price, and an open card checks price and availability every 60 seconds while the page is visible.

Can buyers see inside a locked vehicle?

Yes, if your inventory includes interior photos. Buyers can browse those photos and the rest of the gallery, enlarge images and read the vehicle description without waiting for someone to unlock the car.

Can a buyer contact the dealership from the card?

Yes. Cards displays call and email links when your dealership's phone number and email are present in the inventory. It can also link to your dealership website. Buyers use their own phone or email app.

Do buyers need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and open the vehicle card in a web browser. An internet connection is required.

How much does Postiqo Cards cost?

Cards is included at no additional charge for a dealership with at least two active Postiqo Publisher subscriptions. There are no extra Cards fees or commissions, and Cards is not sold separately. Two Publisher accounts cost C$298 per month in total. See Publisher pricing.

What happens when a vehicle is no longer listed?

When the synchronized inventory marks a vehicle inactive, the card displays "No longer listed" and hides the asking price. Visitors can still contact the dealership for current availability.
