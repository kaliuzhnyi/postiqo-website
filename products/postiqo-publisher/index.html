How we calculate it

90 vehicles / 7 days x 30 days x (7 + 1 + 1 minutes) / 60 = 57.9 manual hours per month.

Manual cost = these hours x your hourly rate. Estimated savings = manual cost minus the Postiqo subscription. Negative savings mean the subscription costs more at your inputs.

Questions dealership owners and managers ask

Find out how to try Postiqo with your own vehicles and fit it into your inventory process.

Can I try Postiqo with my own vehicles?

Yes. Download Postiqo for Windows, import your inventory, and preview a vehicle listing for free, without a Facebook login or license. When you are ready to publish, sign in to Facebook in the app and request a 7-day trial. Our team activates access and confirms by email. The trial starts at activation.

How much does Postiqo cost for a dealership?

Publisher costs C$149 per Facebook Marketplace account per month for 1-4 accounts. Two accounts cost C$298 per month and include Craigslist publishing and Postiqo Digital Cards at no extra cost. With 5+ accounts, volume pricing is C$129 per account per month for every account, with both products included. Five accounts cost C$645 per month. All prices are in Canadian dollars. A 7-day free trial and setup are included, with no long-term contract.

How much listing time could my team save?

Enter your vehicle inventory and hourly staff cost in the calculator above. It estimates an ongoing listing rotation, including publishing, finding and removing old ads, and group sharing, then compares the manual labour cost with a Postiqo subscription. You can adjust the cycle and task times. The figures are estimates, not measured customer results.

Does Postiqo renew Facebook Marketplace listings every seven days?

Postiqo can attempt to renew eligible listings after seven days when Facebook makes renewal available. You control renewal and listing lifetime settings. Renewal availability depends on Facebook; a fixed number of renewals or increased reach is not guaranteed.

Does Postiqo run on my computer?

Postiqo Publisher is a Windows desktop app. Keep the app running and the computer connected to the internet for scheduled tasks. Your team controls the inventory source, posting schedule, action limits, and when to pause automation.

Which marketplaces can Postiqo Publisher manage?

Postiqo Publisher automates vehicle listings on Facebook Marketplace: publishing, inventory synchronization, price updates, renewals, reposts, and sold vehicle removal. With 2+ Publisher accounts, Craigslist publishing and Postiqo Digital Cards are included at no extra cost.

Will Postiqo work with my dealership website?

In most cases, yes. Postiqo can import vehicle inventory from public dealer websites built on popular platforms as well as many custom systems. A direct API integration is not required. Contact us with your website address and we’ll confirm compatibility.

How does Postiqo connect to Facebook?

Postiqo works through your existing browser profile, so you sign in to Facebook directly instead of giving Postiqo a Facebook password or API key. Your settings control which posting, updating, sharing, and removal actions can run.

Can I post to multiple Facebook groups at once?

Yes. Add the local Facebook groups your dealership uses, choose when sharing begins, and rotate caption templates. Postiqo then handles the repeat sharing workflow according to your settings.

Does Postiqo use AI to write descriptions?

Yes. Postiqo can generate full vehicle descriptions and shorter group captions using your inventory data. You can customize the instructions to control tone, length, language, and dealership style, or use your own reusable templates.

What is Smart Listing Sync?

Smart Listing Sync checks your source inventory for price, detail, and availability changes. Your rules determine when Postiqo updates a listing and what happens when a vehicle is sold or removed from your dealership website.

How does Postiqo help me control account activity?

Postiqo runs in your browser session and gives you controls for schedules, task frequency, and per-cycle limits. No automation can guarantee how a third-party platform will respond, so dealerships should use reasonable settings and follow applicable Facebook terms and policies.

Can I pause or stop auto-posting anytime?

Yes. Disable the scheduler, pause an account, or turn off selected actions at any time. Postiqo stops new automated tasks while preserving your configuration for when you’re ready to resume.

Can I use different descriptions for different vehicles?

Yes. Create as many named description templates as you need and add optional CEL conditions for make, model, price, or words in the title. For example, use one message for Honda vehicles, another for BMW, and a separate message for AS IS stock. Postiqo randomly chooses one of the matching templates. If none match, it chooses from your general templates, if configured. You can preview the result with your own inventory before publishing.

Can I create branded vehicle price sheets?

Yes. Use the included price sheet design or import a DOCX template with your own logo, colours, layout, and optional vehicle photos. Edit templates in Word or Google Docs and save them as DOCX. Select up to 200 vehicles and export a PDF with one page per vehicle, ready to review and print. PDF generation uses a local LibreOffice document engine: use an existing installation or download the optional engine from the app.
