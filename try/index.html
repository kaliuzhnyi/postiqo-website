YOUR FIRST STEPS WITH POSTIQO

Try Postiqo
at your own pace.

Download the app and see your own vehicles in Postiqo.

STEP 1

Download the app.

02

STEP 2

See your inventory in Postiqo.

The setup guide opens when you first set up an instance. Start by choosing your inventory source in Settings > Data and entering your dealership website or file details.

  • Select Import Inventory to save your source settings and run one full import.
  • Check the result, including how many vehicles were added or updated. Select View inventory to review their details and photos.
  • Select a vehicle and open its listing preview to see your photos, price, and description before publishing.

The guide saves your progress. Close it whenever you like and select Open setup guide on the Welcome page to continue. You can adjust templates and schedules later in Settings.

03

STEP 3

Request your trial in the app.

Continue in the setup guide, or open the Welcome page when you're ready to publish.

  1. Select Sign in to Facebook and sign in with the personal account you plan to publish from.
  2. Select Request a free trial. Your connected Facebook account is included automatically.
  3. Enter your contact and dealership details, then select Request my 7-day trial.

Your dealership website is required. Postiqo fills it from your source settings when available, and you can edit it before sending.

Activated by our team

Your first publication

  1. We review your request and activate trial access for your Facebook account.
  2. You receive an email confirming your trial is ready.
  3. Postiqo checks for activation while the app is open. You can also select Check activation to refresh your license status.
  4. Once your license is active, return to the setup guide and select Publish to Facebook to publish the vehicle you previewed. Requesting a trial does not publish anything.
  5. When available, select Open Facebook listing in the success message to view your post.

Your publishing trial gives you 7 days of free use from activation. You can keep importing and reviewing vehicles while you wait.

Prefer to request your trial on the website?

This form is an optional alternative to the request in Postiqo. Enter the personal Facebook account you will use in the app. All fields are required except the additional notes.

Use the personal Facebook account you will use in Postiqo. This is the account we will add to your license.

Where to find it

  1. Sign in to the correct Facebook account in your browser.
  2. Open facebook.com/me (opens in a new tab) and wait for your profile to load.
  3. Copy the resulting address from the address bar and paste it above.

The easiest option: paste the full profile address into the Facebook field above. You can also enter just the number or username, as shown below.

Example 1: your address contains a number
facebook.com/profile.php?id=123456789
Your user ID is 123456789.
Enter: 123456789

Example 2: your address contains a username
facebook.com/john.smith
Your username is john.smith.
Enter: john.smith in the same Facebook field.

These are examples. Use the number or username from your own profile address, including any dots or numbers. The facebook.com/me shortcut itself is not your user ID.

Please send your profile ID or link, never your Facebook password.

By sending this request, you ask us to contact you about your trial and use your Facebook ID to set up your license.

Sending your trial request...
Your trial request has been sent. We'll email you when your trial is active. Sign in to that Facebook account in Postiqo and select Check activation. Once your license is active, you can start publishing.

Your 7-day publishing trial starts when our team activates it. We'll confirm by email.
