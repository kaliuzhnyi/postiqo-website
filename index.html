A few useful answers

Getting to know Postiqo.

What does Postiqo do for car dealerships?

Postiqo gives dealers two ways to use their inventory. Publisher automates vehicle listings on Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist. Cards turns a printed QR code into a mobile vehicle page with photos, current inventory pricing and dealer contacts.

Which product should I start with?

Start with Publisher to automate your listing work. You can import inventory and preview listings for free, then request a 7-day publishing trial in the Windows app. If your dealership has two or more active Publisher subscriptions, ask us to enable Cards at no additional charge.

Can I buy Postiqo Cards on its own?

Cards is available as an included benefit for dealerships with at least two active Publisher subscriptions. It is not sold separately. There are no additional Cards fees or commissions.

Will Postiqo work with my dealership inventory?

Publisher can import inventory from supported dealership websites and other compatible sources. Share your website with us and we will check compatibility before you commit.
